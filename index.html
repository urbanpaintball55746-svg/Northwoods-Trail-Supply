Built for the country outside Hibbing.

The company is based in Hibbing, on the Mesabi Iron Range. The work around it is practical: shelters that pitch in wet pine duff, lights that start at dawn, dry bags for a portage, and recovery gear for a two-track after rain.

Listings carry a house SKU, the advertised MAP retail, and the spec lines a buyer in Ely or a distributor reviewer in another state can read before writing in. Field photographs on this page are atmosphere. They are not staged claims that a particular frame was shot in our yard.
